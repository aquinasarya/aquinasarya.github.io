How I work

Over 6+ years I have designed telco dashboards, museum systems, SaaS products, AR exhibitions and brand websites. I work end to end: research, flows, design systems, prototypes, testing and hand-off. I also build the front end for many of my own designs.

I started at the Apple Developer Academy with the Muara App for Museum Nasional Indonesia. Today I am Managing Partner at Muara Studio, and I design products for Providea and Albarsha Group.
